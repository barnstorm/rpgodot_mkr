# Asset licence

The art and sounds in `assets/starter/` (characters, tiles, faces, monsters, backdrops, titles,
window skin, the generated icon sheets `IconSet24.png` / `IconSet32.png`, music and sound effects)
are generated by `tools/generate_starter_assets.gd` and `tools/generate_icons.gd`. They are
dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required.

Everything else in this repository is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

This repository contains no RPG Maker files. The importer copies RPG Maker art and audio from
folders you own into your own project; those files stay under the RPG Maker EULA. The MIDI
renderer can use Windows' `gm.dls` (Microsoft's) or an SF2 soundfont you choose; neither is
included here.
